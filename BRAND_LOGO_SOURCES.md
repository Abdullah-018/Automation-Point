- Wikimedia Commons: Inditex, Pull&Bear, Kohl's, JCPenney, Fruit of the Loom, Primark, MAX Fashion, New Look, LC Waikiki, Levi's, GAP, PEPCO, Tommy Hilfiger and LPP.
- Simple Icons / theSVG: ZARA, Tesco, H&M, Auchan, UNIQLO and Walmart.
- Official brand sites: Centric Brands, Perry Ellis and U.S. Polo Assn.
- Logos Download: CCC.

Sources were retrieved in August 2026. The website stores local copies so its presentation does not depend on third-party logo CDNs.
